# User Guide

Welcome to the Alumni Knowledge Network! This guide explains how to use the platform based on your role.

## Getting Started (All Users)

1. **Login:** Click "Sign in with Google" on the login page.
2. **First-Time Setup:** On your first login, you will be asked to complete your profile. Select your role (Student or Alumni), your field of study/expertise, and add relevant skills/tags.
3. **Navigation:** Use the sidebar to navigate between the Feed, Search, Alumni, Leaderboard, and your Profile.

## Student Guide

Students use the platform to learn, discover mentors, and ask questions.

- **Universal Feed:** View the latest knowledge posts and questions. Use the tabs to filter by "Information" or "Question".
- **Search:** Find specific topics, tags, or people using the search bar.
- **Alumni Discovery:** Browse the "Alumni" page to find mentors and experts in your field.
- **Posting:** You can create "Question" posts to ask the community for help. Make sure to tag relevant fields and skills.
- **Commenting:** Reply to posts to ask follow-up questions or share your thoughts.
- **Voting:** Upvote helpful posts and comments to reward the author with an Authority Score.

## Alumni Guide

Alumni use the platform to share knowledge, answer questions, and build their reputation.

- **Knowledge Sharing:** Create "Information" posts to share industry insights, advice, or technical knowledge.
- **Answering Questions:** Browse the feed for "Question" posts in your field and provide helpful answers in the comments.
- **Building Authority:** When your posts and comments receive upvotes, your Authority Score increases. 
- **Earning Badges:** As you contribute more, you will earn badges that display on your profile and next to your name.
- **Leaderboard:** Check the Leaderboard to see the top contributors in the community.

## Moderator Guide

Moderators keep the community safe, organized, and high-quality.

- **User Management:** Access the "Moderation" tab to view all users. You can block users who violate community guidelines. Blocked users cannot post or comment.
- **Content Moderation:** You have the ability to delete inappropriate posts or comments directly from the feed or post view.
- **Analytics:** View basic platform metrics (total users, total posts) on the Moderation dashboard to understand platform activity.

## Admin Guide

Admins have full control over the platform and system settings.

- **Role Assignment:** Admins can promote other users to Moderator or Admin roles.
- **Override Actions:** Admins can unblock users or restore access if a moderation action needs to be reversed.
- **Platform Control:** Admins have all the capabilities of a Moderator, plus highest-level system access.
