# Post and Comment Quality Rules

## Information Post Standards
A quality Information post should:
- Have a clear, specific title that describes what is being shared
- Provide practical, actionable content based on real experience or verified knowledge
- Be scoped to one topic or takeaway (not a broad essay)
- Use the correct field and relevant tags for discoverability
- Credit sources or experiences honestly

Examples of good Information posts:
- "5 things I wish I knew before my first software engineering job"
- "How I passed the Google technical interview — my preparation strategy"

## Question Post Standards
A quality Question post should:
- Ask a specific, clear question in the title
- Provide context in the body (what the student already knows, what they have tried)
- Be tagged with the relevant field and topic
- Tag an alumni if the question is directed at a specific person's expertise

Examples of good Question posts:
- "What should I focus on when studying for AWS Solutions Architect?"
- "Is a CS degree required to work in data science? I have a Math background."

## Comment Standards
Comments should:
- Directly address the post or a previous comment
- Be constructive and respectful
- Add new information or a new perspective, not just agree or disagree
- Avoid one-word or low-effort replies

## Tagging Guidance
- Tag alumni only when the content is genuinely relevant to their expertise
- Use existing tags before creating new ones
- Keep tags concise and lowercase (e.g. internship, not Internship Tips 2024)

## Moderation Triggers
Posts or comments may be moderated if they:
- Contain offensive, discriminatory, or harassing content
- Are spam or unrelated to academic or professional topics
- Tag users maliciously or without relevance
- Violate community trust through misinformation
