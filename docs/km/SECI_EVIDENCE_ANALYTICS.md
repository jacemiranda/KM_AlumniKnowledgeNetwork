# SECI Evidence and Analytics Notes

## SECI Evidence Summary (For Oral Defense)

### Socialization
- Evidence: Posts and comment threads where alumni and students exchange
  practical knowledge directly
- Metric: Total comment count, comment depth per post

### Externalization
- Evidence: Information posts authored by alumni capturing tacit career
  and academic experience in written form
- Metric: Total Information posts, posts per field

### Combination
- Evidence: Search results, field/tag filtering, leaderboard rankings
  demonstrating organized, retrievable knowledge
- Metric: Unique fields used, unique tags created, search usage

### Internalization
- Evidence: Student authority scores, badge awards, and leaderboard
  positions showing growth from consuming and applying alumni knowledge
- Metric: Total badges awarded, authority score distribution

## Analytics Available in MVP
Basic analytics visible to Admin and Moderator on the User Management page:

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| Total users | Count of all registered profiles |
| Total students | Count of profiles with user_type = student |
| Total alumni | Count of profiles with user_type = alumni |
| Total moderators | Count of profiles with role = moderator |
| Total admins | Count of profiles with role = admin |
| Current online | Approximate count of active sessions (if session tracking is available) |

## Analytics Interpretation Notes
- A high student-to-alumni ratio is normal and expected; alumni are the knowledge source
- A high post count with low comment count may signal that discussions are not forming —
  consider prompting engagement through the alumni page
- A high downvote rate on a user may warrant moderator review
- Badge distribution across fields shows which domains are most active in knowledge sharing

## Defense Notes
- The SECI model is not just a label — every core MVP feature maps to at least one phase
- The platform deliberately avoids private messaging to keep knowledge capture public and reusable
- Authority score and badges are the primary Internalization signals in the MVP
